## Do not mistake build success for a bootable kernel

This result is a **compiler + final linker + DTB** milestone. It does NOT test hardware runtime ACPM mailbox/firmware, PMIC rail voltages or interrupts, I²C Speedy, battery charging, UFS host PHY/calibration/blocks, screen, Wi-Fi, cameras, Android 16 ramdisk/init, vendor HALs or SELinux at runtime. ACPM drivers include initcalls that are not validated as safe to execute on this phone. Therefore the successful P17 \`vmlinux\` MUST NOT be packaged or flashed.
