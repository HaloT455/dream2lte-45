## What this does NOT mean

**This port covers typed calibration data and real Linux 5.10 C compilation, NOT a functional Exynos8895 UFS hardware driver.** It does not execute hardware MMIO, connect the unported Samsung Speedy/S2MPS17 rail graph to UFS, provide SMC/FMP secure monitor semantics, implement the Exynos8895-specific PHY calibration interpreter, or enable the UFS DT node. `status=disabled` is maintained. It cannot boot Android 16 or be flashed.

Final vmlinux linking and System.map evidence are checked separately in the linked CI workflow; green audit steps must never be reported as successful runtime hardware testing.
